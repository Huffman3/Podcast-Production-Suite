# Anti-AI Writing Style Guide

Goal: produce raw, conversational, human-centric writing. Avoid corporate, overly polished, or predictable structures.

## Forbidden Words (Never Use)

delve, leverage, harness, robust, landscape, utilize, realm, transformative, game-changer, gamechanger, seamless, testament, leveraging, harnessing, utilizing

## Forbidden Phrases (Never Use)

- "It's important to note…"
- "In today's digital age…"
- "Unpack…" (as a verb for explaining something)
- "At the end of the day…"
- "Furthermore" / "Moreover"
- "It goes without saying…"
- "In conclusion…"
- "Let that sink in."
- "In summary…" / "To summarize…"

## Sentence Structure

Mix short and long sentences. Vary rhythm. Never write three sentences in a row at the same length.

Never start consecutive sentences with the same word — not "The," not "This," not "AI," not any word.

Contractions are good. "It's," "won't," "can't," "you're" — use them freely. They signal a human wrote this.

## Tone

Write like a person texting a knowledgeable friend. Opinionated. Direct. No filler. A one-sentence paragraph is fine. Don't over-explain.

Avoid corporate jargon. Avoid overly professional platitudes. Include hesitation or subjectivity where genuine — that's what humans do.

## Structure Rules

- Paragraphs: keep them uneven. Short is fine. Long is fine. Mix them.
- Lists: only when the content is genuinely list-like. Not as a crutch.
- Endings: don't summarize. Don't wrap up neatly. Just stop when you're done.

## Staccato Patterns to Avoid

Do not use: "No X. No Y." constructions.
Do not use: "It's not X, it's Y." constructions.

These are overused and immediately read as AI-generated or copywriter-template content.

## How to Apply This Skill

When rewriting or editing:
1. Scan for every forbidden word and phrase — cut or replace each one
2. Read aloud mentally — if it sounds like a LinkedIn post, rewrite it
3. Vary sentence length until no two consecutive sentences feel the same
4. Check sentence openers — break any consecutive-same-word patterns
5. Remove any summary or conclusion paragraph
6. Read the result — if it still sounds corporate, it needs another pass

See `references/style-rules.md` for extended examples and before/after rewrites.
