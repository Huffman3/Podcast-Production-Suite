# Podcast Production Suite

Everything a podcaster needs. In one plugin.

From first research note to final sponsorship invoice — one toolkit handles it all.

Built by **Slo Burn Media** for *Who Killed…?* and independent podcasters everywhere.

---

## What's Inside

### Production
| Tool | What It Does |
|------|-------------|
| **Production Pipeline** | Track every case from discovery to published episode. Never lose a story again. |
| **Podcast Research Agent** | AI-powered case discovery. Find the story, the angle, and the sources — fast. |
| **Audio to Transcript** | Drop in an MP3. Get back a timestamped, speaker-labeled .docx in minutes. |

### Guest Work
| Tool | What It Does |
|------|-------------|
| **Guest Outreach** | Cold emails that open doors. Calibrated by guest type. Initial + follow-up, ready to send. |
| **Guest CRM** | Manage your entire guest pipeline — outreach, prep, and follow-up — in one place. |

### QA & Polish
| Tool | What It Does |
|------|-------------|
| **Fact Check Skill** | Verify every claim before you record. Catch gaps, audit voice, protect your credibility. |
| **Anti-AI Checker** | Paste your draft. Get a Human Score. Rewrite every flag before it goes to air. |

### Launch & Promote
| Tool | What It Does |
|------|-------------|
| **Marketing Newsletter** | Turn your latest episode into a polished newsletter. One click, broadcast-ready. |

### Revenue & Analytics
| Tool | What It Does |
|------|-------------|
| **Episode Performance** | See how every episode lands across platforms. Know what's working and why. |
| **Monthly Revenue Chart** | Visualize sponsorship trends month by month. Know your number at a glance. |
| **Sponsorship Tracker** | Log deals, track invoices, and never miss a deliverable. Your ad ops in one view. |

### Full Suite
| Tool | What It Does |
|------|-------------|
| **Suite Hub** | Your production command center. All tools, live data, one dashboard. |

## Commands

- `/transcribe` — Drop in an MP3 and get a timestamped, speaker-labeled .docx transcript
- `/check-writing` — Audit any text for AI-sounding language and rewrite it in your voice
- `/setup` — Guided tour of all tools in the suite

---

## Getting Started

Run `/setup` after installing for a guided overview of every tool and when to use it.
