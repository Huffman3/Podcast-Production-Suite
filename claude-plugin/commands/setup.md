Welcome the user to the Podcast Production Suite and give them a clear, scannable orientation.

Present the following tools in plain language — no jargon, no file paths:

## Dashboards & Artifacts
- **Set-up Wizard** — This guided overview of everything in the suite
- **Podcast Research Agent** — AI-powered case discovery and research dashboard
- **Production Pipeline** — Track every case from discovery to published episode
- **Anti-AI Checker** — Scan any text for AI-sounding language and rewrite it to sound human
- **Marketing Newsletter** — Generate and send your podcast marketing newsletter
- **Episode Performance** — Track how each episode is performing across platforms
- **Monthly Revenue Chart** — Visualize sponsorship and revenue trends month by month
- **Guest CRM** — Manage your guest pipeline: outreach, prep, and follow-up

## Skills (AI-Powered Workflows)
- **Audio to Transcript** — Convert any MP3 into a timestamped, speaker-labeled .docx transcript
- **Guest Outreach** — Write cold outreach emails (initial + follow-up) tailored by guest type
- **Fact Check Skill** — Verify claims, audit voice, and catch gaps before recording

After presenting this overview, ask: "Which of these would you like to try first, or do you have something specific you're working on?"
