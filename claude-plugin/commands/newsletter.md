description: Write a marketing newsletter for this episode
tools: [Read, Write]
---

Turn a podcast episode into a subscriber-ready marketing newsletter.

1. Ask the user for the following if not already provided:
   - Episode title or case name
   - Episode brief, script, or show notes (paste or reference)
   - Target audience tone (professional, conversational, true crime enthusiast, etc.)
   - Any specific promotion angle (new investigation, guest interview, update on known case, etc.)

2. Read the provided episode material and extract:
   - Hook/teaser (most compelling 1-2 sentences)
   - Episode summary (2-3 sentences, case-focused, not show-focused)
   - Key reveals, suspects, or investigative turns
   - Any guest information or special content
   - Call-to-action (listen on platform, share, reply with tips, etc.)

3. Write the newsletter in .docx format with:
   - Subject line (compelling, not clickbait)
   - Greeting (casual, warm)
   - Teaser/hook paragraph
   - Body (2-3 paragraphs summarizing the case and episode angle)
   - Guest or expert info (if applicable)
   - Call-to-action (link to episode, social share, or engagement request)
   - Footer (unsubscribe, social links, brief branding)

4. Deliver the finished .docx as copy-paste ready for Mailchimp, Substack, Beehiiv, or any email platform

5. Offer revisions if tone or length needs adjustment
